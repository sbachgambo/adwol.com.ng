01 / CLIENT

Federal Ministry of Livestock Development

A Federal Government ministry, one of the public-sector clients ADWOL serves.

02 / CONTRACTOR

ADWOL Investment & Services Limited

Responsible for the full scope, from procurement and logistics to commissioning.

03 / PROJECT

250kVA Solar Power System

Procurement and installation of a complete solar power system, including panels, inverter and battery systems.

SEC. 02 — DELIVERY

Four Stages, One Accountable Team

The work ran through four connected stages. The photographs are from the site.

STAGE 01

Procurement & Preparation

Equipment and materials were sourced and moved to site, with logistics and site preparation handled by ADWOL so installation could begin on solid ground.

STAGE 02

Installation

Solar panels, mounting structures, cabling, inverter and battery systems, and all associated installation activities.

STAGE 03

System Configuration & Integration

Electrical connections, configuration and integration of the panels, inverter and battery storage into one working power system.

STAGE 04

Testing & Commissioning

Testing, verification and commissioning of the completed system before handover.
