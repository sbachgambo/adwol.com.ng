SEC. 03 — NON-OIL EXPORTS

Non-Oil Pre-shipment Inspection

Nigeria's non-oil exports compete on trust. Buyers abroad want proof that what they ordered is what will arrive — in the right quantity, to the agreed quality, properly packed and correctly documented. ADWOL provides independent pre-shipment inspection for non-oil exports, verifying consignments before they leave the country so exporters ship with confidence and buyers receive exactly what they contracted for.

Our inspection teams work at the point of origin — aggregation points, warehouses, processing facilities and loading yards — and follow each consignment through to sealing and dispatch. Every inspection ends in a clear, documented report that the exporter can put in front of a buyer, a bank or a regulator.

  • Exporters of agricultural commodities, processed foods and manufactured goods who need independent verification before shipment.
  • Buyers and trading houses commissioning an impartial check on goods purchased from Nigerian suppliers.
  • Cooperatives and aggregators consolidating produce from many suppliers into export-ready lots.

Inspection workflow
  1. 01

    Request & scoping

    We agree the commodity, quantity, applicable standards, inspection location and timeline with you before any team is deployed.

  2. 02

    Inspection at origin

    Inspectors attend the site and verify each lot against the contract, packing list and specification.

  3. 03

    Sampling & testing

    Representative samples are drawn and sealed, with laboratory testing arranged where a buyer or destination market requires it.

  4. 04

    Loading supervision & sealing

    We supervise loading, record container and lot details, and confirm the consignment is sealed as inspected.

  5. 05

    Reporting

    A structured inspection report records findings and supporting evidence, with lot and container references you can share with your buyer.

Quantity & Weight

Physical count and weight verification against the contract, packing list and invoice, with tallying at the point of loading.

Quality & Specification

Visual and physical checks against agreed specifications, with representative sampling and coordination of laboratory analysis where required.

Packaging, Marking & Documents

Inspection of packaging condition, labelling and shipping marks, plus a consistency review of supporting documents before the consignment ships.
