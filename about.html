About ADWOL

Committed to delivering first-class services to public and private sectors since 2018.

SEC. 01 — PROFILE

Who We Are

ADWOL Investment & Services Ltd (RC 1509051) was established in 2018 to deliver first-class services across Investments, Consulting, Advisory, Trading, Procurement and General Contracting. What began as a focused investment and advisory practice has grown into a multidisciplinary firm trusted across construction, energy, technology and public-sector procurement.

We serve Federal Government MDAs, Corporate Organizations and Private Clients under a single standard: quality, budget and schedule, without compromise. Rather than hand a project between departments, one accountable team carries each engagement from initial scoping through delivery and after-service support — the same discipline whether the brief is a multi-week advisory review or a year-long federal construction contract.

  • Technical Experts: World-class professionals with continuous training in best practices.
  • Innovation: Young, creatively driven teams that go beyond standards.
  • Owned Equipment: Company-held plant and machinery — including dozers, dump trucks, motor graders and generating sets — mobilized directly to site rather than subcontracted.
  • Values: Teamwork, Professionalism, Innovation & Value-Adding.
